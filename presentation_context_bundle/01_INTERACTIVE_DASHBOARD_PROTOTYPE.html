Comparison of ReLU, GELU, SiLU, Tanh, and LeakyReLU across all 7 forecast lead times on held-out test split.

LeakyReLU (α = 0.2) - Champion Robustness

Maintains continuous non-zero slope for negative pre-activations. Eradicates dying ReLU neurons when extreme cold advection anomalies sweep across the Iberian Peninsula. Achieves consistent double-digit skill advantages across the entire multi-day forecast envelope.

Hyperbolic Tanh - Best Short-Range Diurnal Model

Produces an extraordinary +19.68% error reduction at +16h (dropping RMSE by 1.18 K). Bounded range (-1, 1) perfectly captures cyclical diurnal insolation, but slightly saturates under multi-day synoptic extrema.
